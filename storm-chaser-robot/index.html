Storm Chaser Robot

Help the robot deliver packages through changing weather and around trees and rocks. Find the route with the fewest moves. If a shorter route exists, you get to try again!

How to play

  • Move one tile at a time with the arrow keys or W A S D.
  • Trees and rocks block the way. Walk around them.
  • Rain makes the robot slow. Each step onto rain costs 2 moves.
  • Wind blows the robot one tile in the arrow's direction. Moving against the arrow pushes through the gust: that step costs 2 moves, and the robot is not blown.
  • +1+2 rainFeedback: a green +1 is a clean step. Red labels and flashes show what the weather cost you.
